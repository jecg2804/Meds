## Procedure / timeline articles
- Art. 16: digital file (plataforma tecnológica / expediente digital).
- Art. 17: procedimiento regular = technical + analytical evaluation.
- Art. 18: abreviado = registered & marketed in countries with high-standard authority recognized by WHO/PAHO, set by executive decree.
- Art. 19: application filed by lawyer, refrendo of pharmacist + Colegio Nacional de Farmacéuticos; notarized power to lawyer.
- Art. 21: after documentary approval, satisfactory lab results needed before payment order ("orden de pago de liquidación por derecho de registro"); abbreviated: no prior lab tests.
- Art. 22: deficiencies -> 3 months to cure, else returned (new application).
- Art. 24: abbreviated requirements (form; sworn decl.; CPP from NRA per high-standard-country decree, or CLV+GMP; formula; method; CoA; specs; stability; sample/labels; monograph).
- Art. 26: reference labs set fees for evaluation of manufacturer's own method validation reports.
- Art. 28: CLV/CPP (WHO-type) original/authenticated, apostille, Spanish translation, <=2 yrs old.
- Art. 29: GMP certificate validity 2 years unless stated.
- Art. 32: multisource products with high-standard SmPC: no clinical studies.
- Art. 34: samples: one original sample to DNFD + quantity required by reference lab; same lot as lab sample.
- Art. 39: abbreviated route products may be marketed while post-registration QC analysis is done (within 6 months).
- Art. 43: Colegio Nacional de Farmacéuticos sets its own refrendo fee.
- Art. 44: before RS certificate issued -> pay "tasa correspondiente al Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas" (amount not stated in DE 27).
- Art. 160/164: RS validity 5 years (stated for natural products; FAQ DNFD Oct-2025 confirms 5 yrs for medicines).
- Art. 212: imports under exception: shelf life >= 18 months; Art. 214: exception valid 60 business days.
- Art. 219: no modifications within 6 months of expiry.
- Art. 221: modifications implemented within 12 months.
- Art. 223: modification deficiencies cure 6 months.
- Art. 248-251: control previo (pre-registration lab analysis) for all first-time registrations EXCEPT abbreviated route and local production; paid directly to reference lab/authorized lab.
- Art. 255-256: abbreviated route: within 6 months must arrange QC analysis of first imported lot; 5 calendar days to present quote/payment.
- Art. 257: must designate RCPR (Responsable de Controles Post-Registro) pharmacist (may be the regente).
- Art. 262: post-registration QC costs paid to reference lab within 15 days of notice.
- Art. 270: 3 months to deliver reference standards.
- Art. 364: DNFD up to 3 business days to evaluate import applications.
- Art. 366: imported product shelf life >= 6 months at entry.
- Art. 369-371: Certificación de importación al amparo de RS vigente (parallel importer) + control previo first lot (Art. 370).
- Art. 376/398: licencia de operación valid 2 years.
- Art. 377(3): Agencia Distribuidora (A/DNFD) = import/export/storage/distribution wholesale; includes logistics centers.
- Art. 382(3): regencia for import/storage/wholesale distribution: may be < full day, MINIMUM 4 hours daily, declared. Without regente (dismissal) license suspended; other causes 30 business days to replace.
- Art. 385: license requirements (power, form, Registro Público cert <=6 months, IDs, regente's other-establishment schedule letter if regente works elsewhere -> i.e., shared regencia contemplated; floor plan with m2; fee).
- Art. 385(2): renewal requires Aviso de Operación copy.
- Art. 396: 20 business days to cure license observations.
- Art. 397: pre-opening inspection; regente must be present.
- Art. 399: renew 1 month before expiry.

## Therapeutic equivalence (DE 27 arts. 64-99)
- Art. 66: public entities buying generics must require interchangeability for molecules listed by MINSA resolution (Ley 419 art. 62).
- Art. 69: TE applies to chemical-synthesis Rx drugs; NOT OTC, biologics, orphan.
- Art. 74: products already registered get until next renewal.
- Art. 75: abbreviated interchangeability route for products approved as BE/TE/interchangeable by FDA, Health Canada, EMA, EU NRAs, WHO PQ, Swissmedic, PMDA, TGA, MHRA.
- Art. 84: in vivo required for BCS II/IV, suspensions, parenteral non-aqueous, MR, FDCs, NTI etc.
- Art. 92(5)(c): 18-24 subjects typical.
- Art. 95: biowaivers: BCS I, BCS III (very rapid dissolution), additional strengths, IVIVC, SUPAC; not NTI.
- Art. 96: adopts FDA/EMA/WHO/Health Canada BE guides.
- Art. 97: NO TE studies for: aqueous parenteral solutions (IV/IM/SC/intrathecal), oral solutions, gases, powders for reconstitution as aqueous parenteral/oral SOLUTION, aqueous ophthalmic/otic solutions, aqueous topical solutions, aqueous inhalation/nasal solutions.
