# Ley 419 / DE 2-2025 / DE 12-2025 / DE 29-2023 / Res. 985-2025 — FACTS (fetched 2026-10-05)

## Ley 419 de 1-feb-2024 (GO 29962-A, jueves 01 feb 2024)
URL: https://dnfd.minsa.gob.pa/sites/default/files/2025-08/Ley%20419%20de%201%20de%20febrero%20de%202024.pdf
(HTTP 200, 8,103,132 bytes, 86 pp, scanned -> OCR tesseract eng psm4 @250dpi; OCR text: downloads/dnfd_docs/ocr_l419/all.txt)
NOTE: this is the ORIGINAL text; it does not reflect Ley 462 de 18-mar-2025 amendments (DE 16-2026 in project confirms Ley 462 modified Ley 419, e.g., art. 264 via art. 241 of Ley 462).
- Art. 19: MINSA regulates DNFD service fees (tasas) by regulation.
- Art. 27: annual fee review indexed to CPI medicinal/pharma products (INEC).
- Art. 29: RS application via lawyer, form, refrendo of pharmacist + Colegio Nacional de Farmacéuticos; requirements incl. WHO-type CPP (or CLV + GMP of each site), manufacturing contract when applicable, formula, monograph, validated methods (RTCA), specs, labels, stability per RTCA, safety/efficacy studies, analytical standards, samples, one specimen, fee receipt.
- Art. 31: interchangeability evidence basic requirement for critical/serious disease drugs on DNFD list.
- Art. 33: Spanish or official translation; clinical studies in English OK with Spanish summary.
- Art. 39: certificate names manufacturer & country; third-party manufacturers must appear on RS.
- Art. 41: DNFD issues RS within <=20 business days after receiving satisfactory lab analysis (and complete docs); reference labs have <=60 business days to issue analysis result.
- Art. 42: priority for innovators with special fee.
- Art. 43: "Registro sanitario de productos fabricados por terceros": product made by lab in a country where it is NOT marketed, on behalf of lab in another country -> (1) certification from health authority of manufacturing country that product is manufactured but not marketed there; (2) GMP certificate of producing lab from that authority; (3) other DNFD requirements; label must show manufacturer & country of origin.
- Art. 48: RS validity 5 years; renewal: can keep selling if renewal filed >=3 months before expiry.
- Art. 49: fuente alterna: RS issued within <=30 calendar days after satisfactory analysis.
- Art. 53: renewal any time before expiry; 6-month grace to keep number (no sales).
- Art. 54: abbreviated: no prior lab analysis if registered AND marketed in high-standard countries pre-qualified by WHO/PAHO recognized by executive decree; post-registration QC of first imported lot within 6 months.
- Art. 55: locally made: no prior analysis; first-lot analysis.
- Art. 58-62: interchangeability; art. 62: public institutions buying generics on interchangeability lists must meet criteria; exception possible when RS exception authorized.
- Art. 66: prior analysis mandatory except exceptions; Art. 67: at least TWO post-registration analyses during RS validity; costs paid directly to lab within <=5 business days of order.
- Art. 72: reference labs = Instituto Especializado de Análisis (IEA, Universidad de Panamá) and INDICASAT-AIP; DNFD can authorize others.
- Art. 74: reference labs must complete control previo within <=60 business days from sample receipt.
- Art. 76: tests not doable locally -> foreign labs authorized; costs to manufacturer.
- Art. 79: IEA and INDICASAT set fees per analysis; must communicate to DNFD within 30 calendar days of law's entry into force and MAKE THEM PUBLIC.
- Art. 80: samples, documents, standards and analysis fee paid directly at the reference/authorized lab.
- Art. 82: Universidad de Panamá and INDICASAT set fees for their services.
- Art. 86: regente must verify received products have >=12 months shelf life when applicable.
- Art. 94-96: import under existing RS (parallel import) -> certification; importer pays fee + control previo first lot + post controls.
- Art. 97: licensed establishments under responsibility of a regente farmacéutico.
- Art. 100: regente legally responsible for technical operations.
- Art. 267 (amends art. 10 Ley 24/1963): license valid up to 2 years.
- Art. 268 (amends art. 19 Ley 24/1963): "Un mismo farmacéutico no podrá regentar, en un mismo horario, dos o más establecimientos farmacéuticos, exceptuando los centros logísticos donde se permitirán hasta seis regencias."

## DE 2 de 7-ene-2025 (GO 30195-A, 13-ene-2025) — WLA recognition ("reconocimiento automático")
URL: https://dnfd.minsa.gob.pa/sites/default/files/2025-08/Decreto%20Ejecutivo%20No.%202%20de%207%20de%20enero%20de%202025.pdf (text layer)
- Art 1: automatic recognition for products manufactured AND registered in countries with WLA authorities; no prior lab analysis if registered & marketed in WLA country.
- Art 2: (a) fee = "tasa por servicio de expedición de registro sanitario por trámite abreviado" (= B/.750 per DE 27 art 15); (b) FADDI form via lawyer + pharmacist + Colegio refrendo; (c),(d) legalized powers; (e) original CPP from country of origin, legalized/apostilled; (f) legalized copy of full dossier filed in reference country; (g) stability zone IVb when required; (h) country of origin consistent. "Los productos que requieran estudios de bioequivalencia quedan sujeto a la normativa vigente de cada país."
- Art 3: response within 10 business days; Art 4: single observation round; Art 5: 5 business days to cure (amended to 10 by DE 12-2025); Art 7: RS number assigned within max 10 business days from receipt/correction AND MEF payment.
- Also: DE 4 de 5-feb-2025 modified art. 1 (not downloaded; referenced in DE 12-2025).

## DE 12 de 25-jun-2025 (GO 30309-A, 26-jun-2025) — amends arts 1, 2, 5 of DE 2-2025
URL: https://dnfd.minsa.gob.pa/sites/default/files/2026-01/Decreto%20Ejecutivo%2012%20de%2025%20de%20junio%20de%202025.pdf (scanned; OCR)
- Art 1 (new): recognition for products REGISTERED in WLA countries (drops "fabricados" -> manufacturing country no longer needs to be WLA); no prior lab analysis if registered in WLA country (drops "comercializado").
- Art 2 (new): (e) copy of CLV or any document proving marketing authorization in a WLA country, legalized/apostilled; (f) legalized/apostilled copy of RS approved by WLA authority; (g) FADDI country of origin consistent with CPP; (h) simple copies: GMP certificate (legalized/apostilled), stability zone IVb, formula, labels, CoA, specs, monograph, insert, sample, analytical method, validation if non-pharmacopoeial.
- Art 5 (new): 10 business days to cure.
- IMPLICATION (my inference, flag as estimate): an Indian-manufactured generic that holds a marketing authorization from a WLA authority (e.g., US FDA ANDA, EMA/EU NRA, Swissmedic, Health Canada, MHRA? [check WLA list]) could use the WLA route even though India/CDSCO is not WLA. An India-only registered generic cannot.

## DE 29 de 28-jun-2023 (GO 29813-C) — abbreviated route + high-standard country list (still referenced as "Decreto Ejecutivo que reconoce los países con autoridades reguladoras de alto estándar")
URL: https://dnfd.minsa.gob.pa/sites/default/files/2026-03/Decreto_ejecutivo_no_29_de_28_de_junio_de_2023_-_requisitos_para_solicitud_registro_sanitario_medicamentos_abreviado_-_normativa.pdf (scanned; OCR)
- Art 2 list: SRAs (Germany BfArM/PEI, Australia TGA, Austria, Belgium, Canada, Cyprus, Denmark, Spain, USA FDA, EMA, Finland, France, Ireland, Iceland, Italy, Japan PMDA, Liechtenstein, Norway, Netherlands, Portugal, UK MHRA, Korea MFDS, Singapore HSA, Sweden, Switzerland).
  WHO-prequalified FOR VACCINES ONLY: China NMPA, Egypt EDA, **India CDSCO**, Indonesia, Serbia, South Africa SAHPRA, Thailand, Vietnam.
  PAHO reference (chemical synthesis): Argentina ANMAT, Brazil ANVISA, Chile ISP, Colombia INVIMA, Cuba CECMED, Mexico COFEPRIS.
- Abbreviated route only if registered AND marketed in those countries.
- => India CDSCO qualifies only for vaccines, NOT for chemical generics. India-only generics -> ordinary route with control previo (lab analysis).

## Resolución 985 de 3-jul-2025 (GO 30317, martes 08-jul-2025) — mandatory TE list (173 actives)
URL: https://www.minsa.gob.pa/sites/default/files/general/resolucion_985_go30317_08072025.pdf (hidden OCR layer + my own OCR + visual check of p.4)
- Repeals Res. 385 and 386 de 9-jun-2023. TE mandatory at registration application for listed actives (Art. 2). Products in process/registered with <=1 yr left: until next renewal (Arts 3-4).
- DNFD FAQ (9-oct-2025): for public tenders, BE demonstration needed to participate even during transition.
  URL: https://dnfd.minsa.gob.pa/sites/default/files/2026-02/Seminario_Virtual_Preguntas_frecuentes_Bioequivalencia_9OCT2025.pdf
- Molecules from the brief, checked against Res. 985 text (pdftotext + own OCR + visual):
  LISTED: levofloxacino (#99; ref Elequine/Janssen, Tavanic/Sanofi), omeprazol (#124; Losec/AZ), metformina (#111; Glisulin/Merck; also sitagliptina+metformina #150), amlodipina (#8; Norvasc/Pfizer), atorvastatina (#12; Lipitor/Pfizer).
  NOT LISTED: paracetamol, amoxicilina/clavulánico, ceftriaxona, cefotaxima, albendazol, mebendazol, nistatina, hidrocortisona, quetiapina, topiramato, clorfeniramina, loperamida, losartán.
- Note DE 27 art. 97 exemptions apply even for listed molecules: e.g., levofloxacin IV aqueous solution exempt; omeprazole IV powder for reconstitution as solution exempt (art 97(4)); only oral solid forms need BE/biowaiver.
