# Lab fees, backlog, regente pay, WLA list — FACTS + ESTIMATES (fetched 2026-10-05)

## (b) Laboratory analysis — Instituto Especializado de Análisis (IEA, Universidad de Panamá)
FACTS
- Ley 419 art. 72: IEA + INDICASAT are the reference labs; art. 79: they must set and PUBLISH fees per analysis (communicate to DNFD within 30 calendar days of the law's entry into force); art. 80: samples/docs/standards and analysis fee paid directly at the lab; art. 74: control previo within <=60 business days of sample receipt; art. 41: DNFD issues RS within <=20 business days after satisfactory analysis.
- DE 27 art. 15: "Análisis para control de calidad pre y post registro" -> "Tasa fijada por el Laboratorio de Referencia de Análisis". Art. 26: separate lab fee for evaluating validation reports of non-pharmacopoeial (in-house) methods.
- IEA website (https://instespecializadoanalisis.up.ac.pa/node/85, fetched 2026-10-05) publishes NO price list; quotes are case-by-case via forms ("Formulario de Solicitud de Evaluación de Expediente para Cotización de Análisis de Control Previo/Renovación/modificación"; "Formulario de Validación"). /descargables returns 404.
- ONLY official number found: MINSA/DNFD "Guía sobre el procedimiento para el registro sanitario" (Josip De Lora, version 26/07/2012): "El Costo del análisis va de B/. 200.00 a 1000.00 dependiendo del tipo de producto y variantes..."; Colegio Nacional de Farmacéuticos refrendo "Costo: B/. 20.00"; tasa por servicio then B/.200; "Costo del pago para el MEF: B/. 25.00 (productos extranjeros) y B/. 10.00 (productos nacionales)"; "habilitado con B/.8.00" (lawyer's application stamp). 
  URL: https://www.minsa.gob.pa/sites/default/files/publicacion-general/guia_sobre_el_procedimiento_para_el_registro_sanitario.pdf  (2012 — STALE; DNFD fee rose to B/.500 under DE 27; IEA fees likely higher now -> treat as floor)
- Number of samples: not fixed in law; DE 27 art. 34: 1 original sample for DNFD + "la cantidad de muestras de análisis requerida por el laboratorio de referencia"; IEA communicates number of samples in its quote (2012 guide step I.1). Same lot as DNFD sample.
- Post-registration: Ley 419 art. 67: at least TWO post-registration analyses during the 5-year RS validity, paid by holder within 5 business days of order. Abbreviated/WLA route: first imported lot analysis within 6 months (Ley 419 art. 54; DE 27 art. 256).
- IEA volume 2025: DNFD list "CONTROLES DE ANÁLISIS Preregistro Sanitario realizados en el IEA – 2025" (to Dec): 193 report IDs (192 unique), 45 entries with India as country of origin.
  URL: https://dnfd.minsa.gob.pa/sites/default/files/2026-02/CC%20pre%20registro%20recibidos%20del%20IEA%20x%20Mes%202025%20dic.pdf
  Post-registration 2025 list: https://dnfd.minsa.gob.pa/sites/default/files/2026-02/CC%20post%20registro%20recibidos%20del%20IEA%20x%20Mes%202025.pdf (only ~ a dozen per month at most; includes Indian products e.g., Apex Drug House furosemide, Medibios clopidogrel)
- B/.1,500 "Formula revision fees University of Panama (for every registry of medication)" in the law-firm table: NO official source found for a fixed B/.1,500 "formula revision" fee. There is no "formula revision" fee in Ley 419/DE 27. The closest real items are (i) IEA control previo analysis (case-by-case quote; 2012 official range B/.200–1,000) and (ii) IEA evaluation of method validation reports (DE 27 art. 26, case-by-case). Abbreviated/WLA-route products have NO prior analysis -> charging B/.1,500 per product on those routes would be unjustified at filing (post-registration first-lot analysis is due within 6 months though).
- UP Consejo Administrativo Res. 2-24 SGP (7-feb-2024) authorizes IEA US$4,000 WHO proficiency testing — no tariffs. URL: https://secretariageneral.up.ac.pa/sites/secretariageneral/files/2024-02/C_Adm_N%C2%B02-24.pdf

## (c) Statutory times (FACTS)
- Ordinary route: lab <=60 business days (Ley 419 arts 41,74) + DNFD <=20 business days after satisfactory analysis (art 41); cure periods: 3 months (DE 27 art 22).
- WLA recognition route: 10 business days response (DE 2 art 3); single observation round; 10 business days to cure (DE 12-2025); RS number within max 10 business days of receipt/correction + MEF payment (DE 2 art 7).
- Fuente alterna: <=30 calendar days after satisfactory analysis (Ley 419 art 49).
- Operating license: no fixed DNFD decision deadline found; 20 business days to cure observations (DE 27 art 396); pre-opening inspection (art 397); valid 2 years (art 398); renew 1 month before expiry.
- Registro Nacional de Oferentes (MINSA): Commission processes within <=30 business days (DE 27 art 726). Requirements: Aviso de Operación, licencia de operación, product catalog, financial statements certified by CPA (DE 27 art 725; Ley 419 art 185).
- Import authorization: DNFD up to 3 business days (DE 27 art 364).
- BPA (Buenas Prácticas de Almacenamiento, Distribución y Transporte) certificate: Res. 181 de 14-ago-2025 (GO 30359-B, 5-sep-2025): valid 2 years; first-time pass thresholds 95% critical / 90% major / 90% minor; CAPA within 30 calendar days; follow-up inspection within 6 months; fee B/.100 (DE 27 art 15).
  URL: https://dnfd.minsa.gob.pa/sites/default/files/2026-02/Resolucion%20181%20de%2014%20de%20agosto%20de%202025%20procedimiento%20de%20BPA.pdf

## (e) Regente farmacéutico
FACTS
- DE 27 art. 382(3): importers/wholesale distributors: regencia may be < full day, MIN 4 hours/day, declared.
- Ley 419 art. 268 (amends art. 19 Ley 24/1963): a pharmacist cannot be regent of 2+ establishments in the SAME schedule, except logistics centers where up to SIX regencias allowed. DE 27 art. 385(1)(e): if regente works at another establishment, schedule certification letter required -> shared regencia across different time slots is contemplated.
- DE 27 art. 382: dismissal without regente -> license suspended; other causes: 30 business days to replace.
- DE 27 art. 257: RCPR (post-registration control responsible) can be the regente.
- Pay: no Colegio minimum-fee schedule found (Colegio site unreachable). Law-firm table (project file) says regente "$400–500 monthly" (part-time, implied).
- Market proxy: Paylab Panama "Pharmacist" (Medicine & Social Care): 80% earn 16,068–38,736 PAB/yr gross (≈1,339–3,228/month); <20 verified respondents. URL: https://www.paylab.com/pa/salaryinfo/medicine-social-care/pharmacist?lang=en (fetched 2026-10-05). Pharmaceutical industry average 24,976 PAB/yr.
- DE 27 art. 8: public DNFD pharmacists get 40% exclusivity bonus (not relevant to private pay).
ESTIMATE: 4h/day part-time regente ≈ 40–60% of a full-time ~B/.1,500–2,500 -> B/.600–1,500/month; B/.400–500 is at/below the low end (plausible only for a regente shared across several clients in a logistics center).
