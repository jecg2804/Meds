## Per-product official cost floor (FACTS + one stale data point)
- Ordinary route (India-only generic): DNFD 500 + MEF ~25 (2012) + Colegio Farmacéuticos ~20 (2012) + IEA analysis 200–1,000 (2012; quote needed) ≈ B/.745–1,545, plus B/.400 interchangeability certificate if molecule on Res. 985 list (and the BE study cost).
- WLA route (Indian product with US/EU/UK/CH/CA/AU/JP/KR/SG approval): DNFD 750 + MEF + Colegio; no prior analysis; first-lot post-registration analysis within 6 months (IEA quote).
- Quote's per-product total: 600 + 750 + 1,500 + 500 + 50 + 25 = B/.3,425 per product (before ITBMS on the 600 fee).

## Additions (verified later on 2026-10-05)
- DGI Tasa Única for S.A.: B/.300 per year (late surcharge B/.50; after 3 unpaid years suspension + B/.1,000 rehabilitation fine). URL: https://dgi.mef.gob.pa/Preguntas/TasaU.php
- DGI RUC inscription for legal entities: "Trámite gratuito" (online via e-Tax 2.0). URL: https://dgi.mef.gob.pa/Preguntas/ruc.php
- DNFD online tariff page (https://dnfd.minsa.gob.pa/index.php/tarifa_tramites) labels the distributor license "Licencia Anual de Operación de Distribución, Laboratorio y Droguería B/.200", while DE 27 art. 15/376/398 say the B/.200 license is "por dos años" and valid 2 years. The decree (legal instrument) controls; flag the inconsistency. The online list omits the BPA certificate (B/.100) and the import-under-third-party-RS certification (B/.500) lines that DE 27 art. 15 contains; it adds "Certificado de Inscripción de Producto Alergénico B/.250" not in DE 27.
- No 2025–2026 decree amending DE 27 art. 15 fees found in MINSA's decree listing (pages 1–3 checked: only DE 4-2026 on food/veterinary fees). MINSA resolution listing pages 0–8 checked: no fee changes; Res. 985 is the TE list.
