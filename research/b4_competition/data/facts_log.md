## Legal thresholds (medicines) – Ley 419 of 1-Feb-2024 (Gaceta 29962-A) & DE 27 of 10-May-2024 (Gaceta 30028-C)
- F | Ley 419 (OCR of official PDF downloaded by sibling task b5 from DNFD site; Gaceta Oficial 29962-A p.58): modalities for health products: 1) Compra menor ágil up to B/.50,000; 2) Contratación menor up to B/.70,000; 3) Licitación pública from B/.70,000; 4) Licitación por mejor valor from B/.500,000 + high complexity; 5) subasta en reversa; 6) licitación de precio único; 7) convenio marco. Fianza de propuesta required only if reference price > B/.70,000 (art. 245). Desierto causes (art. 241): no bidders; no bid compliant; bids riesgosas/onerosas/gravosas; all bids from same economic group; contrary to public interest. Precio único: price is determinant if all requirements met; commission report within 15 business days (+5); award may go to one or more bidders; winners may improve price during vigencia (art. 214). Publication lead time: ≥4 business days if ≤B/.70k; ≥5 if B/.70k–500k; ≥15 if >B/.500k.
  Source file: /tmp/.../research/b5_regulatory/downloads/dnfd_docs/Ley_419_de_1_de_febrero_de_2024.pdf (OCR all.txt lines ~3655-3672, 3964-4090, 4588-4610, 4646-4660). Original URL per sibling b5 notes (DNFD site).
- F | DE 27/2024 arts. 775-779: compra menor ágil B/.1,000–50,000 via cotización en línea, min 4 hours to quote, lowest compliant price wins, single bidder may win if price convenient; contratación menor ≤B/.70,000 via cotización en línea, lowest compliant price; local producer preference: local wins if ≤5% above lowest global price (art. 776; Ley 419 art. 256); if no quotes after two cotizaciones en línea, entity may solicit quotes by other means (art. 778). Proposals rejected outright only for missing/insufficient fianza de propuesta (art. 767). Two bids from same economic group => both void (art. 766). Subsanación ≤3 business days, never for documents not submitted (art. 772). Onerosa = price far from reference price (arts. 769, 789). Homologation meeting mandatory if reference > B/.500,000 (art. 762).
  Source: sibling download /tmp/.../research/b5_regulatory/downloads/de27/de27.txt (Gaceta Oficial Digital 30028-C, 10-May-2024, pp.158-168)
